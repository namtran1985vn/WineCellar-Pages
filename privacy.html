1. Introduction

WineCellar Holdings, LLC (“Céllare,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information in connection with our website, mobile applications, and member services (the “Service”).

By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, address, and login credentials.
  • Cellar information: bottle records, photographs, purchase invoices, valuations, and notes.
  • Payment information: billing details processed through PCI-DSS compliant providers (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Communications: messages exchanged with our concierge or support teams.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information (browser, operating system, device identifiers).
  • Usage data (pages viewed, features used, approximate location by IP).
  • Cookies and similar technologies, used solely to operate and secure the Service. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Information from third parties

  • Pricing data from auction houses and merchant indices (aggregated, not member-specific).
  • Identity verification through KYC providers when required for high-value features.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To provide concierge support and personalised recommendations.
  • To produce valuation reports, insurance schedules, and other documents at your request.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your cellar with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes — ever.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers bound by contractual confidentiality (cloud hosting, payment processing, email delivery, identity verification).
  • At your direction — for example, when you authorise sharing a schedule with your insurer or advisor.
  • Legal compliance — when required by valid legal process, with notice to you when permitted.
  • Corporate transactions — in the event of a merger or acquisition, with continued protection of your information.

5. Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes. You may request deletion at any time, subject to certain legal exceptions.

6. Security

We protect your information using industry-standard safeguards, including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, hardware-key two-factor authentication, and SOC 2 Type II-audited infrastructure. While no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, we work continuously to maintain rigorous standards.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. California Notice (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have additional rights regarding their personal information, including the right to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To submit a verifiable request, contact us at privacy@winecellar.example.

9. International Members

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States under U.S. law. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms for cross-border transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will post material updates to this Policy and notify members by email at least thirty (30) days before they take effect. Continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
