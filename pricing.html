Questions of the House

Frequently asked.

How does the invitation process work?
Membership is offered through introductions from existing members, partner sommeliers, wealth advisors, or directly by application. Most decisions are returned within seventy-two hours, by phone.
Will my collection ever be visible to others?
Never. Céllare does not display, share, or sell member data. Aggregate market signals are derived only from anonymised transaction data we license externally — never from your cellar.
Can I import an existing CellarTracker or eSommelier database?
Yes. Our onboarding team will personally migrate your existing records — including notes, photographs, and purchase history — at no additional cost on every tier.
Do you take a commission on auction or merchant transactions?
No. Céllare receives no payment from any merchant, auction house, or retailer. Recommendations are independent and member-aligned by design.
How is my data secured?
Bank-grade AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Documents are stored in SOC 2 Type II-audited US data centers. Access requires hardware-key two-factor authentication.
May I cancel at any time?
Of course. Annual memberships may be cancelled at renewal; monthly memberships at the end of any cycle. Your full data export is available always, in industry-standard formats.
