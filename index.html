The Stewardship

Every bottle, attended to.

An exacting set of services designed for the collector who values both provenance and privacy.

Master Cataloguing

A bottle-by-bottle ledger of your holdings — vintage, parcel, format, provenance — captured in moments via photograph or import from leading cellar specialists.

Live Valuation

Real-time market pricing drawn from international auction houses and merchant indices — your collection's worth, refreshed before your morning coffee.

Provenance Vault

Bank-grade encryption protects every certificate, receipt, and chain-of-custody document. Insurance-ready exports prepared at a moment's notice.

Drinking Windows

Sommelier-curated maturity guidance for every bottle, paired with discrete reminders so a 1990 Pétrus is never forgotten on its peak.

Cellar Climate

Optional sensors pair with WineCellar to monitor temperature, humidity, and vibration — alerting you the instant your environment drifts from ideal.

Estate Transfer

Generational planning made graceful. Designate heirs, arrange charitable bequests, and prepare clean records for trusts and estates.

A Considered Approach

Discretion is the first vintage we pour.

WineCellar was founded on the principle that a great collection deserves quiet attention. We do not advertise our members. We do not share their holdings. We do not sell data — ever.

Our team includes Master Sommeliers, fine-wine appraisers, and a board advised by leading auction houses. Every recommendation is offered without commission, every valuation independently sourced.
