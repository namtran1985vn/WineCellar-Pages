Common Questions

Quick answers.

How do I reach the concierge after hours?
Grand Cru and Premier members enjoy priority SMS access twenty-four hours a day. Reserve members may email at any hour and will receive a reply by the following morning, Eastern time.
Can I add bottles by photograph?
Yes. Our label-recognition system identifies most producer/vintage combinations within a second. For unusual or rare bottles, our concierge will catalogue them on your behalf at no charge.
May I generate an insurance schedule on demand?
Always. From your dashboard, choose Documents → Insurance Schedule. Your underwriter will receive a signed PDF formatted to AAIS or ISO standards within minutes.
What if I lose access to my account?
We will verify your identity through your registered phone number and a recovery question of your choosing. Account restoration typically completes within an hour, business days or weekends.
