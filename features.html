Features & Services

The full stewardship.

Every facet of WineCellar is built to honour a serious collection — without ostentation, without compromise.

Master Cataloguing

Bottle-by-bottle records covering producer, cuvée, vintage, format, parcel of origin, location, and acquisition history. Bulk import from CellarTracker, eSommelier, and major retailer accounts.

Live Valuation

Daily mark-to-market pricing aggregated from Christie's, Sotheby's, Hart Davis Hart, Liv-ex, K&L, and merchant indices, with bid/ask spreads and twelve-month performance.

Provenance Vault

Encrypted storage for purchase invoices, négociant certificates, and chain-of-custody documents. Insurance-ready PDF schedules generated on demand.

Drinking Windows

Maturity guidance from Master Sommeliers and major critics — Parker, Galloni, Suckling, Meadows — distilled into a single recommended window for each bottle.

Climate Monitoring

Optional WineCellar Sense™ probes track temperature, humidity, and vibration across multiple zones. Alerts arrive by SMS, email, or your preferred concierge.

Estate Transfer

Designate heirs and bequests, prepare clean trust schedules, and coordinate with your wealth advisor or family office through secure document hand-off.

Auction Liaison

When the time arrives to deaccession, our team negotiates terms with leading auction houses on your behalf — with no commission to WineCellar.

Multi-Cellar

Manage holdings across primary residence, second homes, and bonded storage. Geofenced inventory keeps every location precisely accounted for.

Sommelier on Call

A direct line to certified sommeliers for pairing counsel, acquisition advice, or tasting-note retrieval — discreetly, around the clock.

A Considered Approach

Built by collectors. Advised by Masters.

WineCellar's product council includes three Master Sommeliers, two former heads of fine wine at major auction houses, and a chartered appraiser certified by the American Society of Appraisers.

Every feature passes through a single test: would a serious collector use this twice a week, with pleasure?
